## Critical engineering corrections / watch-outs

1. **Electrical power is the most immediate system-wide risk.** Raspberry Pi official docs specify **1.6 A total for all Pi 5 downstream USB ports with capable 5 A PSU** (otherwise 600 mA). The USB limit is **shared with fan header**. [Official Pi documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html). Historical display claim: 5 V × 0.4 A typ; XVF3800, SSD and speaker load remain unmeasured. A nominal 27W supply is not proof that an unknown attached USB/LED load will work.
2. **LED seller current figures conflict across variants/pages:** some text says 10mA per pixel but other variants specify **up to 60mA per RGB pixel at full-white**. For conservative provisional analysis use **40 × 60mA = 2.4A peak, ~12W** for a 210mm outer-diameter ring, not the 10mA number as a guaranteed cap. It may be substantially lower at limited brightness. Require datasheet, measured actual strip current, brightness limit, protected appropriately rated 5V feed, grounds and logic-level compatibility. Avoid direct Pi GPIO supply of LEDs; Pi signal outputs are 3.3V (evaluate level shifter).
3. **40-mm drivers available locally** is progress, **not proof they fit**. The Hackstore product page contradicts itself on dimensions (Ø40×19 in description vs 78×78×26 metadata). Current 38-mm-class CAD speaker envelopes cannot be treated as compatible. Need dimension/photo/drawing and test of output through fabric, serviceability and acoustic enclosure.
4. **Potential XVF3800 + speaker confusion:** [Seeed FAQ](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/respeaker_xvf3800_faq/) explicitly: the mic module has **no built-in speaker**; it documents 3.5-mm audio and JST interface, but warns not to invent connector pinouts or speaker pairing. Existing handoff phrasing about '5W amplified speakers' is manufacturer language, **not** confirmation that both local passive speakers can connect directly, nor stereo output. Validate exact board variant, firmware, pinout and amplifier architecture first.
5. **CSI cable solves availability, not signal quality:** a 500mm CSI-specific cable is listed locally, while HDMI cables of 0.5m are also offered. Verify plugs and bend radii in Ø36mm aluminium neck **before** claiming full assembly fit, particularly the full-size HDMI connector head.
6. **Do not infer stock or warranty from add-to-cart alone.** Confirm local shipping to user, Israeli VAT-inclusive total, return/warranty, and supplier actual inventory shortly before purchase. Imported mic delivered pricing and customs duties are still unverified. Never order until user approves.
