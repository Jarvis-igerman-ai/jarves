# Mechanical context (deferred)

Target appearance: white compact friendly desktop robot, circular black screen, bright blue eyes/expressions, discreet camera above the screen, mic array at top, two speaker grilles on body, optional light ring, detachable back access panel.

### Enclosure goals
- Prototype envelope only: approx H=280 mm, W=200 mm, D=160 mm; **PROVISIONAL**, not locked.
- 5-inch round screen discussed, external diameter ~150 mm (confirm exact manufacturer SKU/CAD drawing; visual artwork is not proof).
- Separate front/back head shells; body shell, rear service door, removable Pi/electronics tray, two serviceable speaker mounts, separate mic mount, cable routing and cooling vents.
- Prefer accessible M3 fasteners and captive nuts or serviceable threaded inserts. No adhesive-only sealed assembly.
- CAD part IDs stable for assembly/exploded view. Need validated assembly sequence and printed test fixtures before final shell.
- Include future spare internal volume / standard USB expansion slot, but don't implement speculative devices.
- Speaker chambers/acoustic isolation and mic placement must be tested physically: geometry visualization is NOT acoustic proof.
- A nice Blender render is NOT a printable CAD drawing. Keep STEP/STL exports CAD-driven.

### Unknowns to capture later
Exact screen bezel and mount, Pi active cooler height, mic module body and cable bending, speaker cone depth, enclosure wall thickness, PCB fixing holes, power harness, thermal vents, cable cutouts, print shrink/tolerance, printer bed constraints.
