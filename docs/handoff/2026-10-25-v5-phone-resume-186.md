# V5 #186: resume a phone conversation on Windows (25 Oct 2026)

Proved with tests only (no code gap found): `core/tests/test_v5_phone_resume.py` drives a paired emulated phone through the real
`/api/m/chat/send` and `/api/m/chat` endpoints plus a desktop WebChat and the terminal `ChatService`, fake model, no paid call.
Covers: phone to desktop (`/resume` lists it, loads the same history into the model and the page), continue on the desktop, back to
the phone (same model memory, phone sees the PC turns), a restarted desktop resuming a phone conversation, `jarvis chat` resume of a
phone conversation, and a privacy-mode phone conversation refused honestly. One stored conversation, one model history (no second memory).

Not verified: the real Core (`app.Core.respond_remote` logs to the log file, the store ingests asynchronously, so a `/resume` right
after a phone turn could lag by the ingest delay); a real phone browser; `jarvis chat --resume` as a flag does not exist (only the `/resume` command).
