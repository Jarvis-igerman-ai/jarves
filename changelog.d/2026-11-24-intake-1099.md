- Fixed: learned preferences, goals and dev drafts now refuse API keys and tokens (#1099): known token shapes, a key/token word followed by a value ("token: ...", or up to five words later "is/was/הוא/היא/זה" plus a 6+ character word with a digit or a non-hyphen symbol, a bare "key" included), long high-entropy letter+digit segments, and hex secrets (32+ hex characters unless 40 or 64 long, or any such hex next to a key/secret word); "token: <word>" is refused even for a plain word. Merely naming or describing a token ("fix token refresh expiry", "the token is expired", "the token is well-known"), snake_case and camelCase identifiers, UUIDs and 40/64-character git SHAs without a key word stay allowed.
