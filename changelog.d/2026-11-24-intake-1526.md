- Fixed: privacy mode now also scrubs goal plan text written before it was switched on (#1526). When privacy is on at Core start, when it turns on, or after plan text was written while it was off (also between two runner ticks), the plan runner erases older step evidence and tool text in step errors, plan notes and results in one memory.db transaction over the two plan tables. It keeps only statuses, result codes and JARVES's own fixed reasons. It then truncates the write-ahead log. secure_delete is switched on only for that transaction and restored afterwards. If a reader holds the log, only the truncate is retried, with a backoff from 2 s doubling to 300 s; the failure is logged once and never stops a plan. New block and failure reasons and done-plan results written in privacy mode get the same reduction. Free pages left by deletes made before privacy was on are not compacted; this change runs no VACUUM.
