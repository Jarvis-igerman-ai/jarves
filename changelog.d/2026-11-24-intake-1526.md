- Fixed: privacy mode now also scrubs goal plan text written before it was switched on (#1526). With privacy on at Core start, after it turns on, or after tool text was stored while it was off, the plan runner erases older step evidence in one memory.db transaction over the two plan tables, in a worker thread that never stops plans. Step errors and plan notes keep only JARVES's own fixed reasons, cap notes or a whole result code; other text becomes "failed". A done plan's result keeps its `N. tool: status` lines. A failed scrub is retried after a backoff (2 s doubling to 300 s) and logged once per outage. secure_delete is on for that transaction only; older copies already in the write-ahead log or free pages are not forced out (no checkpoint, no VACUUM).
