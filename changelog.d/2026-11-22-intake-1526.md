- Fixed: privacy mode now removes goal plan text written before it was switched on (#1526). At Core start and whenever privacy turns on, the plan executor blanks step evidence and error text and the plan's note and result in one explicit transaction, keeping state, step status and the "N/M steps done." count. The rewritten cells are overwritten (SQLite secure_delete), then memory.db is vacuumed so text in space freed earlier (deleted plans, rewritten rows) is dropped, and the WAL is truncated. None of these steps waits for another process: a locked database or busy WAL is warned once and retried on the next tick until it succeeds.
