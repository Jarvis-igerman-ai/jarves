- Added: WhatsApp collect (beta, off by default). `jarvis whatsapp collect <topic> --from <person>` and the `whatsapp_collect` tool gather every message on a topic to, from or mentioning the named people. Payments match Hebrew synonyms and shekel amounts. The result is a CSV, an Excel file and a Hebrew summary in the reports folder, with totals per payee and rows to check. The chat/voice tool asks the owner on screen first. Reading and the AI labelling share one time budget inside the tool timeout, so the report is still written. AI batches cut by that budget stay flagged rows to check. A payment collect reads each chat in full once instead of also running every search word, so many chats fit the read cap. A non-numeric AI amount is rejected and the local row is kept for a check. Payee names in the answer are labelled as taken from the messages (JAR-273).
