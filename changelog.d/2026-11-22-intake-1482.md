- Fixed: learned preferences, goals, goal plans and development drafts now refuse long hex keys (32+ chars), random 20+ char mixed-case tokens, and hex values of 7+ chars with a digit next to a credential word (English, including glued forms such as apikey or authtoken, or Hebrew). A git commit SHA or a named checksum (sha256/sha512/md5) is still allowed after a word such as commit or sha256, or in a commit URL. A word such as token or webhook earlier in the sentence does not refuse a SHA named by commit, unless ':', '=' or 'is' assigns it. A bare 40-char hex value with six or more digits in a row is still refused, as before (#1482).
