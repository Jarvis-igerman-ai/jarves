- Fixed: security P3 list #206. Privacy mode: the phone resend ledger (`continuity.db`) keeps only listed ids, counts and flags (`ok`, `pref`, `plan`, `conflicts`, `snoozed_until`, `stopped`) and a code/state shaped like a fixed token (letters and `_`, at most 32), not free text, of an answer, never its words or other numbers. Rows written while privacy was off are blanked at the next Core start, or at the first ledger write with privacy on after any process wrote with it off (a mark kept in the database; scrub, write and mark are one transaction; not before every write), and freed pages inside `continuity.db` are overwritten (`secure_delete`); the short-lived rollback journal of that write is deleted, not overwritten. A privacy-mode resend of an error keeps its fixed code and message, or gets a defined "answer not kept" message; a resent success answer gets no error code added, and the internal `not_kept` marker is not sent to the phone. Rewording a confirmed preference on the phone now sets it back to proposed (a confirm racing the edit cannot make the new words active; if the row keeps changing, nothing is saved and the phone gets a 409 `PREF_CHANGED` "try again" message in English and Hebrew). The phone guide says the `status` permission shows whether the computer microphone is in use; the project-manager guide says the check command runs agent code without a sandbox. Not changed: plan step evidence in privacy mode, and the ledger is not cleaned at the moment privacy is switched on (#1395).
