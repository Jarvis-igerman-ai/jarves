- Fixed: when privacy mode is on at Core start, or is switched on later, the plan executor blanks plan text written before it in memory.db (step evidence and error, plan result and note), at most 500 rows per table per transaction and one transaction per tick, and keeps only status and error codes such as `X_BROKE` (only A-Z and "_", with at least one "_"; a code with a digit is blanked too). Freed space is overwritten; after the last chunk the -wal file is checkpointed and truncated; while a reader keeps that checkpoint busy or it (or the journal-mode read before it) raises a database error, PLAN_PRIVACY_CHECKPOINT_BUSY is logged once and only the checkpoint is tried again every 30 s until it succeeds (PLAN_PRIVACY_WAL_TRUNCATED). The scrub does not wait for a lock held elsewhere: it fails at once, is rolled back, logged as PLAN_PRIVACY_SCRUB_FAILED and tried again after 30 s; an unexpected crash is logged as PLAN_PRIVACY_SCRUB_CRASH, backs off the same way and does not stop plans. Rows already scrubbed are not rewritten again (#1526).
