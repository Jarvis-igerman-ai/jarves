- Fixed: confirming a learned preference now carries the version of the wording you were shown (#1514). The dashboard and phone send it. If the wording was edited in between, nothing is confirmed (409 `PREF_CHANGED`): the page reloads and says, in Hebrew or English, that the wording changed. A voice confirm only lands on the exact wording its approval popup showed, and without a popup that can show the wording (no popup, or a plain yes/no prompt) it is refused (`PREF_NOT_SHOWN`). Every change to a preference moves its version forward, even within one clock tick. A phone or dashboard page cached from before this update cannot confirm (400 `PREF_PAGE_OLD`, "reload the page") until it is reloaded. A confirm of a preference removed meanwhile answers "not found", not "changed". The CLI confirm path is tracked in #1550.
