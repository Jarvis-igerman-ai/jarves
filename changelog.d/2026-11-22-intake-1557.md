- Fixed: when the owner switches privacy on (the dashboard/CLI privacy switch, or switching full text off), text stored before it was on is now scrubbed too. Phone chat and goal answers in the continuity command ledger become the privacy-mode note. Goal plan steps lose their evidence, errors keep only their code or fixed reason, and a finished plan's result keeps only its step count. Both databases are then compacted (VACUUM) and their write-ahead log checkpointed, so rows deleted earlier do not keep their words in free pages. A database that cannot be scrubbed, or whose write-ahead log cannot be checkpointed, is reported in the answer, logged as `PRIVACY_SCRUB_FAILED`, and shown as a short note in the dashboard top bar and in the `jarvis privacy full-text off` output until a later switch-on succeeds. The automatic end of a 24 h full-text window does not scrub yet (#1689). (#1557)
