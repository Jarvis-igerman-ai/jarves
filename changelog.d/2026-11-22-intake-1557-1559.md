- Fixed: when privacy mode turns on (dashboard switch, end of a full-text window, a switch made by another JARVES process, or starting JARVES with privacy on in config.toml or the privacy switch file), text stored before it was on is scrubbed too: phone/web command answers in the resend ledger become a fixed "not kept" note, and goal plan steps keep only their status and an error code such as TOOL_ERROR. A database that could not be scrubbed (including a file or folder error), or whose old pages a reader kept in the file, is logged as PRIVACY_SCRUB_PARTIAL, listed under "scrub.partial" in the privacy switch's JSON answer, and the dashboard privacy switch shows a short English/Hebrew note; config.toml is still saved. Stopping JARVES waits briefly for a background scrub (PRIVACY_SCRUB_STILL_RUNNING if it is still busy). Backups made before privacy was on are not changed (#1557). Learned preferences now refuse an authenticator seed next to a seed word (totp, otp, 2fa, mfa, authenticator, Hebrew words with up to three prefix letters) also when it is written in spaced 4-character groups or has no digit (#1559).
