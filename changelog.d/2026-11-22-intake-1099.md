- Fixed: learned preferences, goals and dev drafts now refuse API keys and tokens (#1099): known token shapes, a key/token word followed by a credential-like value ("token: ab12cd34", or up to five words later "is/was/הוא/היא/זה" plus a 6+ character word with a digit or a non-hyphen symbol), and long high-entropy keys even when split by - _ or /. Merely naming a token ("token refresh: fix it", "Tokens: rotate weekly", "the token is expired", a token word before a URL), identifiers, paths, UUIDs and git SHAs stay allowed.
