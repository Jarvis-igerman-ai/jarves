- Fixed: sensitive-text check now refuses an unbroken 16+ char single-case base32 seed that contains a digit 2-7 (not plain hex) within 20 characters of a seed word such as totp, otp, 2fa, mfa, seed, authenticator, secret (so a pasted otpauth://...?secret= URI is caught), otpauth or the Hebrew סיד/מאמת/אימות/אוטנטיקטור, so it is not stored as a preference, goal, goal plan or development draft. Long digit-free words such as INTERNATIONALIZATION stay allowed; a 16+ char identifier with a digit next to a seed word (for example `seed the database with base64urlencoded rows`) is refused on purpose as a fail-safe. Known misses: a digit-free seed and a seed written in spaced groups (#1516).
